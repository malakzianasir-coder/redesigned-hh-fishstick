**Prepared:** 12 August 2026  
**Purpose:** A checklist of pictures, logos, and wording still needed so the public website can go live without placeholders, stock photos, or guesswork.

Please return files by email or shared folder. For photos, JPEG or WebP is fine. Portraits work best as a clear head-and-shoulders shot on a plain background. Department and service photos should show the actual room, equipment, or activity named below — not a similar area of the hospital.

---

## How to use this list

Items are grouped by type. Each item says **where it appears**, **what we need**, and **why**.

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Must have** | Currently blank, a grey placeholder, or a QR/logo box with no real file |
| **Please replace** | Something is on the page, but it is the wrong subject, a stand-in, or a stock photo |
| **Please confirm / write** | Text, names, dates, or a decision — not a photo |
