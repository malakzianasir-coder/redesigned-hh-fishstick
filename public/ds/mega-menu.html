Design System

Header Mega Menu

Reference for the Hijaz Hospital site-header mega menu implemented in React (SiteHeader + UtilityTopBar). Eight top-level sections open full-width dropdowns with grouped links and featured slots — use the live navigation in the site header (desktop xl and up) or the hamburger drawer on smaller screens. The inventory, route mapping, and CMS proposal are below.

Try It

Interaction checklist

Hover & focus parity

Mouse hover and keyboard focus both open the same full-width panel under the header. Only one panel is open at a time.

Esc to close

Pressing Esc closes the open panel and returns focus to its trigger. The active trigger shows a 2px red top border and a rotated chevron.

Featured slots

Clinical Departments and Donate / Ways to Give include a right-side featured card with image slot and a ghost-button link.

Mobile drawer

Below xl, the hamburger opens a full-screen drawer where each section expands as an accordion. Esc closes it and returns focus.

C. CMS-editability proposal

Option A — recommended

Payload navigation global

A new global (same pattern as bankDetails / donationMethods): topLevel[] → label + groups[] → heading + links[] (label + link, reusing src/fields/link), plus optional featured (image, line, link) and optional moreLink per panel. SiteHeader renders the global with a hardcoded snapshot as build-time fallback. Editors can reorder, relabel, and re-point links without a deploy; global versions/drafts give a review flow.

Option B — interim

Hardcoded IA in SiteHeader

The IA above ships as a typed const next to the header component. Zero CMS work and type-safe, but every wording/order/link change requires a code change and deploy, and labels can drift from CMS slugs. Sensible until the IA is approved; this mockup is the spec.

D. Accessibility notes

  • Top-level triggers are real <button>s with aria-expanded, aria-haspopup, and aria-controls; panels are role="region" labelled by their trigger (aria-labelledby).
  • Hover/focus parity: panels open on mouseenter (pointer) and focusin (keyboard) — anything reachable by mouse is reachable by keyboard.
  • Esc closes the open panel and returns focus to its trigger; Esc also closes the mobile drawer (focus returns to the hamburger).
  • Only one panel open at a time; closed panels use visibility: hidden so their links stay out of the tab order.
  • Chevrons (ph-caret-down) are decorative (aria-hidden) and rotate 180° when open; the active trigger also gets a 2px red top border.
  • Visible focus-visible ring (primary-red/40) on triggers, panel links, and drawer controls.
  • Mobile drawer: focus moves to the close button on open, body scroll is locked, and accordions are buttons with aria-expanded + aria-controls.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce disables the 200ms panel/drawer animation and smooth scrolling.
  • Panels are capped at viewport height with internal scroll, so long link lists never trap content below the fold.

E. Gaps — no current CMS route

  • Leadership & Governance — founders, chairpersons, presidents, senior management, executive committee, core working committees need CMS pages (or a grouped page with anchors).
  • Diagnostics — Accreditation / Quality Certificates — no dedicated route; candidate home is a laboratory service page section.
  • Patient Welfare — Dialysis Patient Support, Free Medicines, Free Meals, Sehat Sahulat Program, Forms, Online Services are outside the current patient-welfare slug whitelist (financial-assistance, admission-process, patient-stories, free-medical-camps, patient-rights).
  • Zakat — the zakatCalculator block exists but has no dedicated page or anchored section on /donate yet.
  • Donate cause slugs — sadaqah, eidi-fitrana, donate-a-meal, donate-in-kind, sponsor-a-patient, sponsor-free-surgeries, itw-medical-tower must exist as published donations docs (pattern routes).
  • Our Impact & Donor Wall — mockups only; need proposed pages slugs /our-impact and /donor-wall. /reports already covers Impact Reports.
