Our Philosophy

A Legacy of Faith, Service, and Compassion

Founded on enduring values, Hijaz Hospital has grown from a vision of service into a trusted healthcare institution. Guided by faith, compassion, and professional excellence, we are committed to providing quality healthcare that meets the physical, emotional, and social needs of our patients.
