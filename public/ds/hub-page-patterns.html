Design System

Hub & Content Page Patterns

Same chip rail everywhere. On hubs, on-page chips filter. On innermost content-full pages, those chips scroll to the section — they never hide content. External chips (arrow-square-out, new tab) stay beside the rail on both.

Vision & Mission

Vision & Mission

What we aim for, and how we serve every patient — regardless of means.

Vision

To develop Hijaz Hospital into a state-of-the-art secondary-level healthcare institution that serves all strata of society with excellence and compassion.

Mission

To care for the ailing with compassion, respect, dignity, and professional excellence, while ensuring that financial hardship never stands in the way of receiving care.

Our Journey

Our Journey

Milestones from the mosque to a working hospital — the years that shaped Hijaz.

1968

Trial & Perseverance

When construction of Hijaz Mosque began, an encroachment crew demolished the walls. Neighbours and civic allies stood with the Trust until the work could continue.

1970

Completion of Hijaz Mosque

With the support of Begum Ehsan Qadir, M. Hussain Chaudhry, and Mir Salahuddin, the mosque was completed — the first standing piece of the campus.

1986

Foundation Stone & Dispensary

The hospital’s foundation was laid. A modest dispensary opened so the neighbourhood could receive care while the wards were still being built.

Our Philosophy

Our Philosophy

Care with dignity, regardless of means. The hospital exists so no patient is turned away for lack of money.

Hijaz Hospital was conceived as a trust, not a commercial enterprise. Every programme — Zakat, welfare, free medicines, meals — is an expression of that founding idea: healing is a duty, not a privilege.

Our Approach

Our Approach

How we deliver care day to day — clinically, ethically, and as a neighbour to the city.

Clinical excellence

Specialist departments, diagnostics, and inpatient care under one roof.

Welfare first

Financial assessment with dignity, so treatment is never delayed by paperwork.

Community rooted

A hospital built beside a mosque, for the people who live around it.

Our Values
