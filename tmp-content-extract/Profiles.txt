Haji Inam Elahi Asar (1927–2016)
Founder & Honorary Project Director
Hijaz Hospital
Haji Inam Elahi Asar was born on 15 October 1927 in Chiniot, Pakistan. After completing his matriculation, he began his professional career in Kolkata before returning to Pakistan in 1947. He established his business in Karachi and later moved to Lahore in 1953, where he built a successful business while dedicating his life to humanitarian and welfare work.
Inspired by the teachings and noble character of the Holy Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ), Haji Inam Elahi Asar believed that serving humanity was one of the highest forms of worship. His integrity, generosity, and commitment to social welfare earned him recognition as one of Pakistan's leading businessmen and among the country's highest taxpayers during the peak of his career.
His enduring legacy is Hijaz Hospital, a charitable healthcare institution founded on the principles of compassion, dignity, and service. In a remarkable act of selflessness, he donated virtually all of his personal assets—including his residence—to support the hospital's mission. His vision laid the foundation for a modern healthcare facility committed to providing high-quality medical care, particularly to those who cannot afford it.
In addition to Hijaz Hospital, Haji Inam Elahi Asar served numerous educational, healthcare, and welfare organizations across Pakistan, reflecting his lifelong commitment to community development and public service. His vision and values continue to guide Hijaz Hospital's mission of delivering compassionate, accessible, and quality healthcare.

Mian Abdul Waheed (1931–2010)
Lifetime Chairman
Hijaz Hospital
Mian Abdul Waheed served as the Lifetime Chairman of Hijaz Hospital and played a defining role in its planning, development, and administration alongside Haji Inam Elahi Asar from 1983 onwards. Through his visionary leadership, exceptional administrative skills, and unwavering commitment to humanitarian service, he helped shape Hijaz Hospital into one of Pakistan's leading charitable healthcare institutions.
Renowned for his generosity, compassion, and dedication to public welfare, Mian Abdul Waheed devoted his life to serving humanity. He led numerous fundraising initiatives that strengthened the hospital's growth and sustainability while personally contributing to healthcare and welfare projects. His steadfast commitment ensured that Hijaz Hospital continued to expand its services and provide quality healthcare to those in need.
Beyond the hospital, he actively supported educational, healthcare, and social welfare organizations, serving in various leadership and advisory roles. His lifelong dedication to community service reflected his belief in improving lives through compassion, integrity, and responsible leadership.
Even in his final days, Mian Abdul Waheed remained deeply committed to the progress of Hijaz Hospital and its vision of delivering healthcare that meets international standards. His legacy of selfless service, visionary leadership, and philanthropy continues to inspire the institution and remains an integral part of Hijaz Hospital's mission.
Community Leadership
Throughout his life, Mian Abdul Waheed served and supported numerous charitable and welfare organizations, including Fountain House, Defence Welfare Society, Lions Club, Akhuwat Foundation, charitable hospitals and dispensaries, mosques, old-age homes, and several educational and social welfare institutions.
His legacy of compassion, service, and leadership continues to inspire the mission of Hijaz Hospital.

 
Tasneem Firdous Waheed (19XX-20XX) 
Chairperson
Hijaz Hospital
Following the passing of Mian Abdul Waheed, Mrs. Tasneem Firdous Waheed assumed the leadership of Hijaz Social Welfare Society and continued his vision with dedication, compassion, and a strong commitment to humanitarian service.
Under her leadership, Hijaz Hospital witnessed significant growth in its infrastructure, healthcare services, and welfare initiatives. Between 2011 and 2015, she played a key role in advancing development projects, enhancing medical facilities, expanding specialized healthcare services, and strengthening programs for deserving patients and the wider community.
One of her most cherished visions was the expansion of Hijaz Hospital through the establishment of a modern Medical Tower. She personally led the acquisition of land for this important project and laid its foundation stone, setting the foundation for the hospital’s continued growth and enhanced healthcare services.
Although she did not live to witness the realization of this vision, her commitment and dedication continued to inspire the organization. Following her passing, Mian Asif Waheed, along with the Waheed family, office bearers, and members of Hijaz Social Welfare Society, remained committed to carrying forward her vision.
The Medical Tower represents her enduring legacy of visionary leadership, selfless service, and commitment to improving healthcare for the community. Her values continue to inspire Hijaz Hospital’s mission of providing compassionate, accessible, and quality healthcare.
 
Sohail Iqbal Vohra
President (2004–Present)
Hijaz Hospital
Sohail Iqbal Vohra has been associated with Hijaz Hospital for more than three decades, dedicating his efforts to humanitarian service, charitable healthcare, and community welfare. Since 2004, he has served as the President of Hijaz Hospital, providing leadership while upholding the institution’s founding values of compassion, transparency, and service to humanity.
During his tenure, he has played a key role in strengthening the hospital’s charitable healthcare programs, donor relations, fundraising initiatives, and community outreach efforts, helping expand its capacity to serve thousands of deserving patients.
He has also led and supported major humanitarian relief efforts, including the 2005 Pakistan earthquake response, flood relief and rehabilitation campaigns across Punjab, and healthcare initiatives during the COVID-19 pandemic, providing assistance to vulnerable communities during times of need.
His vision and continued leadership remain central to Hijaz Hospital’s mission of delivering compassionate, accessible, and quality healthcare.

•	Strengthened donor engagement, fundraising initiatives, and welfare programs
