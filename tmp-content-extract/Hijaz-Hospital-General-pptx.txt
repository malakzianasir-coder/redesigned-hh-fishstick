=== SLIDE 22 ===
Haji Inam Elahi Asar
Born in October 1927 in Chiniot, a distinguished social worker with over five decades of dedicated service to humanity
A successful businessman and, during his peak years, among the largest taxpayers in Pakistan
Founder and Honorary Project Director of Hijaz Hospital, a lifelong mission rooted in service and compassion
Contributed over PKR 500 million, including personal properties, towards welfare and charitable causes
Actively associated with more than 30 welfare organisations in leadership and advisory roles
Recommended for a Civil Award by the Hijaz Welfare Society in recognition of his services

VISION

=== SLIDE 24 ===
Mian Abdul Waheed
Born in April 1931 in East Punjab, a distinguished humanitarian known for his integrity, humility, and lifelong commitment to public service
A founding member and principal driving force, alongside Haji Inam Elahi Asar, behind the establishment and development of Hijaz Hospital
From its inception as a dispensary in 1979, he remained deeply involved in the planning, construction, and expansion of the hospital
His leadership and vision were instrumental in transforming Hijaz Hospital into a 200-bedded healthcare facility
Actively associated with Defence Welfare Society, Fountain House, Lions Club, and Akhuwat
Hijaz Hospital today stands as a living testament to his vision, leadership, and service to humanity
Back

=== SLIDE 25 ===
Begum Tasneem Firdous Waheed
Dedicated her life to the service of humanity through Hijaz Hospital
Envisioned the expansion of the hospital and the construction of the state-of-the-art Inam Tasneem Waheed Medical Tower (ITW) as her lifelong dream
Donated a significant portion of the required funds during her lifetime towards the completion of the Medical Tower
Personally laid the foundation stone, marking the beginning of this noble initiative
Though she did not live to witness its completion, the Waheed Family continues her mission with unwavering commitment
She embodied strength, patience, dignity, and kindness, nurturing not only her family but the institution itself with a spirit of motherly care
Her values-driven leadership, compassion, and service continue to inspire generations
