6.	Patient Welfare
Financial and community program
Welfare Shariah Program of Hijaz Hospital
Alhamdulillah, one of the most prominent and unique features of Hijaz Hospital, Gulberg Lahore, is the transparency of its Zakat Management System.
Since the Holy Qur’an has clearly defined the obligation of Zakat as well as the specific categories where Zakat must be spent, Hijaz Hospital has established a proper Zakat Management System (ZMS) under the supervision and guidance of qualified Islamic scholars and respected Muftis. The purpose of this system is to ensure that Zakat funds are utilized strictly in accordance with the teachings of the Qur’an and Sunnah, and are spent only on the medical treatment of poor, needy, and deserving patients.
Under the Zakat Management System, the Zakat received from donors is collected through a Shariah-compliant procedure and kept in a completely separate Zakat account. To determine the eligibility of patients, Hijaz Hospital has established a dedicated Welfare Shariah Department, where a panel of qualified Muftis assesses patients with full care, dignity, and respect for their privacy.
If a patient is declared eligible for Zakat, the expenses of his or her treatment are paid from the Zakat account. Patients who do not fall under the Zakat eligibility criteria are supported through general donations or clinical income. In this way, Hijaz Hospital makes every possible effort to ensure that Zakat is received, safeguarded, and spent only on the direct medical expenses of deserving and needy patients.
The entire Zakat Management System is supervised by our capable Shariah Advisor, in consultation with a three-member Shariah Board consisting of respected Muftis. Their guidance helps ensure that the system remains fully aligned with Shariah principles.
We are deeply grateful to Allah Almighty, who has enabled Hijaz Hospital to implement a Shariah-compliant Zakat Management System. Respected and qualified Muftis have reviewed the system, expressed their satisfaction, and confirmed its compliance with Islamic principles.
Alhamdulillah, Hijaz Hospital, Gulberg Lahore, is a trusted healthcare institution where donors can fulfill their obligation of Zakat with confidence and peace of mind, knowing that their Zakat is being spent in the right Shariah-defined category and on truly deserving patients.
Support Flood Relief Program
From the platform of Hijaz Hospital, we have always stood beside suffering humanity in difficult times. During the floods and natural disasters of 2005, 2011, and 2022, Hijaz Hospital’s team actively served affected communities in Punjab and other areas of Pakistan.
Our support included the construction of more than 50 houses for flood victims during the 2022 floods, contribution towards the construction of mosques, and financial assistance for families affected by floods and related hardships.
Hijaz Hospital believes that serving humanity is not limited to the hospital premises only. Whenever and wherever people are in pain, Hijaz Hospital remains committed to sharing their burden with compassion, care, and dignity.
Free Meal Service for Patients and Attendants
At Hijaz Hospital, patient care goes beyond medical treatment. With the spirit of compassion and service to humanity, Hijaz Hospital provides three-time meals daily to admitted patients and their attendants.
This facility is offered to ensure that patients and their families can focus on treatment and recovery without the additional burden of food expenses. Through this service, Hijaz Hospital continues its mission of serving deserving patients with dignity, care, and kindness.
Pharmacy
Free Medicines for Deserving Patients
At Hijaz Hospital, serving humanity with compassion and dignity is our core mission. The hospital provides 100% free medicines to OPD patients, especially those who are deserving and unable to bear the cost of treatment.
Through its Pharmacy Department, Hijaz Hospital ensures that patients receive prescribed medicines without financial burden. This support covers major departments including Gynae, Dialysis, Medicine, and Surgery.

This initiative reflects Hijaz Hospital’s commitment to ensuring that no deserving patient is deprived of treatment due to financial difficulties. With the support of our valuable donors, we continue to provide free healthcare and medicines to those who need them most.
Dialysis Patients Financial Assistance Program
At Hijaz Hospital, dialysis patients are given special care and support because they often face long-term illness and remain dependent on dialysis treatment for survival. Due to the continuous nature of this disease, many patients are unable to work and face serious financial difficulties.
Keeping this hardship in view, Hijaz Hospital provides special financial assistance to deserving dialysis patients. This support includes a monthly stipend, ration support for their families, and additional help in times of personal difficulty.
Hijaz Hospital also extends support to dialysis patients in important family matters, including assistance for the marriage of their daughters where needed. Through this program, Hijaz Hospital not only provides medical treatment but also shares the social and financial burden of patients with compassion, dignity, and care.
This initiative reflects Hijaz Hospital’s commitment to serving deserving patients beyond treatment and standing with them throughout their difficult journey.
Sehat Sahulat Program
Hijaz Hospital facilitates patients through the Sehat Sahulat Program, supported by government funding, to provide accessible healthcare services to eligible citizens of Pakistan.
Through this program, patients can receive treatment support without the heavy burden of medical expenses. Hijaz Hospital is committed to making quality healthcare accessible, affordable, and compassionate for every deserving patient.
Patient Resources
Patient Admission Process
Patient arrives at the OPD Registration Counter for registration and consultation.
Department-wise token is issued and the patient visits the relevant consultant.
Consultant examines the patient and decides whether admission is required.
If admission is required, the patient is referred to the Sehat Sahulat Program (SSP) Counter for eligibility verification.
SSP staff verifies the patient’s eligibility through the Sehat Sahulat Program system.
If the patient is eligible, the procedure amount is approved in SSP and a referral letter is issued.
The patient is then sent to the IPD Admission Counter for admission processing.
An admission file is created at the IPD Counter.
If surgery or procedure is required, the patient is referred for Anesthesia Fitness Assessment.
The anesthesia consultant examines the patient and declares whether the patient is fit for surgery/procedure.
After fitness clearance, billing and financial approval process is started according to the patient category.
Patient category is confirmed, such as SSP, paid, panel, protocol, or welfare/free patient.
If any pending amount remains, the case is forwarded to the Welfare Shariah Department or Finance Department for approval.
For deserving patients, financial assistance or free treatment approval is processed as per hospital policy.
High-value surgeries above Rs. 50,000 are sent for higher management approval.
After completion of admission formalities, the patient is shifted to the relevant ward.
Treatment, surgery, or medical management is provided as advised by the consultant.
During hospital stay, patient care, medicines, billing, and treatment record are managed through the IPD process.
At the time of discharge, final bill is prepared and discharge summary is issued.
After completion of all required approvals and documentation, the patient is discharged from the hospital.

8. Right to Second Opinion

Our journey
In 1968, when the author initiated the construction of Hijaz Mosque, a truck belonging to the Lahore Encroachment arrived and, within moments, demolished the mosque walls, reducing them to rubble. Upon learning of this incident, Mr. Mir Salahuddin, whose residence was adjacent to the mosque, was deeply distressed. He accompanied me to meet the Trust’s Town Planner, Mr. Irshad Ahmed, and requested his cooperation in the construction of the mosque. However, he categorically stated that he would never allow a mosque to be built during his lifetime, though he would fully support the construction of a park or a fire brigade station. Shortly thereafter, the said Town Planner was suspended.
In 1970, with the wholehearted support of Begum Ehsan Qadir (Principal, Government Girls College), M. Hussain Chaudhry (Chartered Accountant), and the late Mir Salahuddin, the construction of the mosque was successfully completed.
In 1979, the author proposed the establishment of a hospital on the land adjacent to the mosque. Although the proposal was accepted in principle, approvals from the Corporation and the LDA remained elusive for seven years. Eventually, through the efforts of the late Mian Zaka-ur-Rehman and with the facilitation of General Ansari, approval was secured within just seven days.
In 1986, the foundation stone of the hospital was laid by the renowned scholar Dr. Israr Ahmed (RA). On 15 January 1988, my respected elder and close friend, Maulana Muhammad Malik Kandhlavi (RA), inaugurated the dispensary. In the early stages, my long-standing associates—Mian Muhammad Aslam (Chairman, Sargodha Group) and Mahmood Ali Bhatti (Chairman, Punjab Steel)—stood by me as pillars of support.
After the completion of the underground structure, Mian Abdul Waheed single-handedly constructed the ground floor. The first floor was completed with the special cooperation of Chaudhry Muhammad Rasheed, Engineer Rasheed, and Mujahid Butt. The contributions of the Chiniot community and members of the Chamber are also gratefully acknowledged.
On 13 December 1988, Hijaz Hospital Welfare was formally registered. By 1994, medical and surgical equipment worth one Crore rupees was acquired for a building of equivalent value, and an additional property worth one crore rupees was purchased to generate rental income.
In 1993, Sheikh Habibullah, former Chairman of the Pakistan Hardware Merchants Association, assumed the role of General Secretary. In 1995, the Executive Committee was expanded, and regular monthly meetings of the governing body commenced.
Several distinguished individuals contributed selflessly to the hospital’s administration and development, including Sheikh Taimur-ul-Haq, Tauqeer Ahmed Sharifi, Al-Haj Mian Abdul Waheed, Aziz A. Mughal, and Sheikh Khushnood Ahmed. Eminent scholars and intellectuals associated with the institution include Dr. Naseem Hassan Shah, Dr. Ikram H. Syed, Professor Altaf Ali Qureshi, and Chaudhry Muhammad Rasheed Khan.
Dr. M. Afzal Sheikh, Chief Executive of Shalamar Hospital and Medical Advisor to Hijaz Hospital, has proven to be a valuable asset for the institution.

Patient Admission Process
Hijaz Hospital follows a structured admission process to ensure smooth coordination, timely treatment, and quality patient care.
Step 1: OPD Consultation & Assessment
Patients visit the OPD Registration Counter for registration and consultation.
A department-wise token is issued, and the patient meets the relevant consultant.
The consultant evaluates the patient and determines whether admission is required.
Step 2: Admission & Eligibility Verification
If admission is advised, the patient proceeds to the relevant admission desk for further processing.
For patients to be registered under the Sehat Sahulat Program (SSP), eligibility verification and required approvals are completed through the SSP system.
Necessary admission documentation is completed, and the patient file is prepared.
Step 3: Pre-Procedure Assessment (If Required)
Patients requiring surgery or procedures undergo assessment by the Anesthesia Department.
The anesthesia consultant evaluates the patient's fitness for the planned procedure.
Step 4: Financial Assistance & Category Assessment
Shouldn’t this be done before pre procedure assessment
Patient category and financial arrangements are confirmed according to hospital policy.
Eligible patients may receive assistance through the applicable support programs, including welfare and financial assistance services.
Step 5: Admission & Treatment
After completion of admission formalities, the patient is shifted to the relevant ward.
Treatment, surgery, or medical management is provided according to the consultant's advice.
During the hospital stay, patient care, medicines, investigations, and treatment records are managed through the inpatient care system.
Step 6: Discharge Process
After completion of treatment, the final bill and discharge documentation are prepared.
The patient receives a discharge summary and necessary instructions before leaving the hospital.

In a remarkable act of selflessness, he donated virtually all of his personal assets—including his residence—to support the hospital's mission. His vision laid the foundation for a modern healthcare facility committed to providing high-quality medical care, particularly to those who cannot afford it.
In addition to Hijaz Hospital, Haji Inam Elahi Asar served numerous educational, healthcare, and welfare organizations across Pakistan, reflecting his lifelong commitment to community development and public service.
