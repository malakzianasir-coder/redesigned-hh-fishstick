Tamgha-e-Imtiaz – Government of Pakistan
In recognition of his lifelong dedication to serving the sick and underprivileged, the Founder of Hijaz Hospital, Haji Inam Elahi Asar (Late), was awarded the prestigious Tamgha-e-Imtiaz by the Government of Pakistan. Our Health Partners
At Hijaz Hospital, we believe that quality healthcare is strengthened through meaningful partnerships. Our collaborations with leading healthcare institutions, universities, and diagnostic centers enable us to deliver exceptional patient care, advance medical education, and expand access to affordable healthcare for the communities we serve.
Teaching Hospital Partner
Fatima Memorial Hospital, Lahore
Hijaz Hospital proudly maintains a strong academic and clinical affiliation with Fatima Memorial Hospital, Lahore, one of Pakistan's leading teaching hospitals.
As an affiliated clinical training center, we provide practical clinical training for MBBS students, house officers, and postgraduate trainees in key specialties, including General Surgery, Internal Medicine, and Gynaecology. Students gain valuable hands-on experience under the supervision of experienced consultants in a real clinical environment.
The partnership also brings highly qualified consultants from Fatima Memorial Hospital to Hijaz Hospital for regular Super OPD Clinics, providing specialist consultations and expert management of complex cases. When advanced tertiary care is required, patients benefit from timely referrals and continued specialist support.
Together, this affiliation strengthens our shared commitment to excellence in patient care, clinical education, and professional development.

Academic Partners
Qarshi University | Hajvery University
Hijaz Hospital is proud to partner with Qarshi University and Hajvery University to promote healthcare education and professional training.
Through these collaborations, students from various healthcare disciplines receive supervised clinical rotations and practical training across multiple hospital departments. The partnerships also support undergraduate and postgraduate research, dissertations, and thesis projects by providing clinical guidance and academic mentorship.
These collaborations contribute to the development of competent healthcare professionals while supporting our mission of compassionate and accessible healthcare.

Diagnostic Partners
Chughtai Healthcare | Al-Noor Diagnostic Centre | Harmone Laboratory
Hijaz Hospital has established strategic partnerships with Chughtai Healthcare, Al-Noor Diagnostic Centre, and Harmone Laboratory to ensure patients have access to reliable and affordable diagnostic services.
Through these trusted partners, patients can receive advanced investigations—including MRI, CT Scan, Biopsy, and other specialized tests—at specially negotiated rates. For deserving patients supported through our welfare program, these services may also be provided free of cost, ensuring that financial hardship never prevents access to essential diagnostics.
These partnerships reflect our Founder Haji Inam Elahi Asar (Late)'s enduring vision that every patient deserves timely, high-quality healthcare regardless of financial circumstances.

Projects of HH
Inam Tasneem Waheed Medical Tower
The Inam Tasneem Waheed Medical Tower is the flagship expansion project of Hijaz Hospital, named in honor of Haji Inam Elahi Asar (Late), Mian Abdul Waheed (Late), and Mrs. Tasneem Firdous Waheed. It reflects their shared legacy of compassion, leadership, and commitment to serving humanity. While Haji Inam Elahi Asar (Late) and Mian Abdul Waheed (Late) founded Hijaz Hospital, Mrs. Tasneem Firdous Waheed, as Chairperson, played an important role in initiating and advancing this landmark project.
From a humble charitable dispensary, Hijaz Hospital has grown into one of Pakistan's leading charitable healthcare institutions. With the increasing number of patients seeking affordable medical care, existing facilities have become stretched. The Medical Tower is being developed to expand capacity and meet the growing healthcare needs of the community.
Located opposite Hijaz Hospital, the basement plus five-storey (six-level) facility covers approximately 16,000 square feet and will provide additional space for advanced diagnostics, specialized clinical services, modern patient care facilities, and expanded inpatient accommodation.
This expansion carries forward Hijaz Hospital's mission of ensuring that no patient is denied quality healthcare due to limited facilities or financial hardship. With the support of donors and well-wishers, the Medical Tower will enable the hospital to serve more patients with dignity, compassion, and excellence.
