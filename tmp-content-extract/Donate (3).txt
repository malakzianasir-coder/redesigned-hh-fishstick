"Whoever saves one life, it is as if he has saved all mankind."(Qur'an 5:32)
Every act of giving helps save lives. Whether you choose to give Zakat, Sadaqah, General Donation, Eidi, or Fitrana, your generosity enables Hijaz Hospital Trust to provide quality healthcare to deserving patients who cannot afford treatment. Explore the different ways you can support our mission and make a lasting impact.
Zakat
Let Your Zakat Become a Source of Healing
Zakat is a sacred obligation and an opportunity to bring hope to those in need. By giving your Zakat to Hijaz Hospital, you help provide free medical care to deserving patients who cannot afford consultations, medicines, surgeries, dialysis, maternity care, and other essential treatments.
Shariah ComplianceHijaz Hospital Trust maintains separate Zakat accounts and ensures that all Zakat funds are distributed strictly in accordance with Islamic principles. Our Zakat management system is independently certified as Shariah-compliant, providing complete transparency and accountability to our donors.
Your contribution enables Zakat-eligible patients to receive quality healthcare with dignity and compassion.
Calculate Your Zakat
Use our Zakat Calculator to calculate your Zakat before making your donation.

General Donation
Every Donation Makes a Difference
Your generosity helps Hijaz Hospital Trust provide quality healthcare to patients who cannot afford the cost of treatment. Every donation, no matter the amount, helps us deliver compassionate medical care to those who need it most.
General Donations support a wide range of essential services, including consultations, medicines, surgeries, dialysis, maternity care, emergency treatment, diagnostic services, and patient welfare programs. They also provide flexible funding for patients and hospital services that cannot be supported through Zakat, enabling us to respond to the hospital's most urgent needs.
General Donations also help strengthen hospital facilities, purchase medical equipment, and support healthcare programs that improve patient care.
Whether you choose to give once or become a regular donor, your contribution helps us continue our mission of providing accessible, high-quality healthcare with dignity and compassion.
Donate today and help bring healing, hope, and better health to deserving patients.
Sadaqah
Turn Your Sadaqah into Healing and Hope
Beyond Zakat, Sadaqah reflects gratitude, kindness, and compassion. Giving Sadaqah regularly is a beautiful act of worship that brings mercy, blessings (barakah), and hope to those in need.
Sadaqah is a voluntary act of charity that brings you closer to Allah and supports those facing illness and hardship.
At Hijaz Hospital Trust, many patients from underprivileged families cannot afford essential healthcare such as medicines, dialysis, surgeries, maternity care, and diagnostic tests. Your Sadaqah helps provide timely treatment with dignity and care.
Even a small contribution can make a meaningful difference—supporting medicines, treatments, and free care for those in need.
Eidi & Fitrana
Share the Blessings of Eid
Eid is a time of joy, gratitude, and compassion. At Hijaz Hospital Trust, many patients from underprivileged families spend Eid in hospitals, unable to celebrate due to illness and financial hardship.
Your Eidi donation brings comfort and happiness to these patients by helping provide essential care and support during treatment.
Fitrana donations also support deserving patients receiving free medical care, helping provide medicines, meals, and treatment support where needed.
Donate your Eidi and Fitrana to Hijaz Hospital Trust and make Eid brighter for deserving patients.

Good nutrition plays an important role in healing. At Hijaz Hospital Trust, many patients from underprivileged families struggle to afford even a nutritious meal while receiving treatment. Donate in Kind
Support Patient Care Through Essential Supplies
Hijaz Hospital Trust welcomes in-kind donations from individuals, organizations, pharmaceutical companies, medical suppliers, and other generous supporters. Your contribution of essential medical supplies, equipment, and other assets helps us provide quality healthcare to deserving patients.
We welcome donations of:
Medicines and life-saving drugs
Medical and surgical equipment
Hospital beds, wheelchairs, stretchers, and other patient-care items
Diagnostic and laboratory equipment
Surgical disposables and medical supplies
Real estate or property donations (residential or commercial)
Other essential items that support patient care and hospital operations
Donated property may be used by Hijaz Hospital Trust for its charitable purposes or sold, with proceeds directed toward patient care and hospital services.
All deserving patients at Hijaz Hospital Trust receive treatment free of charge. Your in-kind donation helps reduce treatment costs, strengthen our healthcare services, and ensure patients continue to receive timely, safe, and compassionate care.
Donate in kind and help provide the resources that make healing possible.
If you wish to donate your property or give any other donation in kind, please contact our Donations Office to confirm the donation requirements.
Sponsor a Patient
Become a Source of Healing and Hope
At Hijaz Hospital Trust, you can directly support the treatment of deserving patients who cannot afford essential healthcare. Your sponsorship helps provide medicines, dialysis, vaccinations, maternity care, and other medical services to those in need.
By sponsoring a patient, you become part of their healing journey—bringing relief, dignity, and hope to individuals and families facing illness and financial hardship.
Treatment Sponsorship Options
Hepatitis Treatment: Sponsor vaccination or treatment support
Dialysis Care: Sponsor single sessions, monthly, yearly, or long-term support
Gynae & Maternity Care: Sponsor maternal care up to delivery
Medical Treatment Based on Patient Need: Support patients based on medical need and treatment plan
Every sponsorship brings a patient one step closer to recovery. Sponsor a patient today.
For details regarding sponsorship options and estimated treatment costs, donors may contact our Donations Office or Hijaz Hospital Reception.

Support Life-Changing Surgical Care
Many patients at Hijaz Hospital Trust require surgery but are unable to afford the cost. Through your support, we are able to provide safe, timely, and free surgical care to deserving patients.
Your contribution helps restore health, reduce suffering, and bring hope to families in need.
Surgical Sponsorship Options
General Surgery: Sponsor surgery for a deserving patient
Urology Surgery: Support urology-related surgical procedures
