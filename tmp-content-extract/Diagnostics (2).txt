Radiology & Imaging
Advanced Imaging. Accurate Diagnosis.
The Radiology & Imaging Department at Hijaz Hospital provides comprehensive diagnostic imaging services that support the early detection, accurate diagnosis, and ongoing monitoring of a wide range of medical conditions. Serving outpatient (OPD), inpatient (IPD), and emergency patients, the department combines experienced radiologists with modern imaging technology to deliver high-quality images and timely, clinically reliable reports.
Our Radiology & Imaging Services
Hijaz Hospital offers a comprehensive range of diagnostic imaging services, including:
Digital X-Ray
General Radiography
Chest X-Ray
Musculoskeletal X-Ray
Ultrasound
Color Doppler Studies
Abdominal Imaging
Obstetric & Gynecological Ultrasound
Image-guided diagnostic procedures (where clinically indicated)
Our ultrasound services support the diagnosis of obstetric, gynecological, abdominal, urinary, vascular, and other medical conditions. Digital X-ray services are available for both routine and emergency patients, providing fast and reliable diagnostic support. When advanced imaging is clinically required, including CT Scan and MRI, patients are conveniently referred through our collaborations with accredited diagnostic imaging centers, ensuring seamless continuity of care.
Advanced Imaging Services Through Trusted Diagnostic Partners
For investigations that require highly specialized imaging, Hijaz Hospital facilitates convenient access through collaborations with accredited diagnostic imaging centers.
These services include:
Computed Tomography (CT Scan)
Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)
Other advanced imaging studies, when clinically indicated
Our clinical team coordinates referrals, facilitates timely appointments, and ensures that diagnostic reports are promptly shared with the treating physician, providing seamless continuity of care.
